## What is this study?

We are checking how closely an automated marking assistant (AnswerWise AI) agrees with teachers when
marking short descriptive answers. **The tool does not decide your marks.** Your marks for the course are
given by your teacher in the usual way, whether or not you take part.

## What would you do?

Allow us to use your written answers to `<questions / assessment>`. Nothing else is required of you.

## What happens to your answers?

* Your name, student ID, email address and anything else that identifies you are removed before the answers
  are marked for the study. Answers are labelled with a random code.
* Two teachers mark each answer independently with the same rubric. The tool then scores the same answers, and we
  compare its scores with the teachers' marks.
* Only `<roles>` can see the answers. They are stored in `<location>` and are not shared outside the study team.
* Raw answers and marks are deleted on `<date>`. Only summary statistics (for example, average differences)
  are kept or published. No individual answer is quoted in publications without separate permission.

## Taking part is voluntary

* Saying no, or saying nothing, has **no effect on your marks or on how you are treated**.
* You can withdraw until `<date, e.g. the day the evaluation set is frozen>` by contacting `<role / channel>`.
  After that date, answers cannot be removed because they are already part of the frozen data. They are still
  deleted on the retention date.
